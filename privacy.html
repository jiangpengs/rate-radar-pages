RateRadarPrivacy Policy

Effective date:

This Privacy Policy explains how RateRadar (the "App") handles information when you use it. We have designed the App so that it does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account, does not show advertising, and does not include analytics or tracking software. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
  • The App only stores on your device the data needed to work: your recently used currency pair and a local cache of the last exchange rates, so the App works offline.

2. How the App gets exchange rates

To show live rates, the App requests exchange-rate data from the European Central Bank (via the public Frankfurter API) when you are online. This request contains only the currency codes you selected (for example, EUR and USD). No personal information is sent with this request.

3. Third-party services

The App uses a public exchange-rate data source to provide its core function. This source receives only the currency codes you choose and does not receive any personal data from you. Beyond this, the App does not share data with any third party.

4. Data security

Because the App does not collect personal information, there is no personal data stored on our servers or elsewhere to protect. All app data lives locally on your device.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights and choices

You are in full control. Deleting the App removes all locally stored data from your device. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing further for you to request or for us to delete.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here with a new effective date. We will not reduce your privacy protections without notice.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or the App, contact us at: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com

Note: This policy reflects the App's current behavior. If a future version of the App adds accounts, analytics, advertising, or data collection, this policy must be updated accordingly, along with the App Store privacy labels.
