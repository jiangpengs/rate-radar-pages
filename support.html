Frequently asked questions

  • Where does the exchange rate data come from?

    RateRadar uses exchange rates published daily by the European Central Bank (ECB). Rates are refreshed each business day when the source updates them.

  • Does RateRadar work offline?

    Yes. Once you have fetched rates while online, the most recent rates are cached on your device and remain available offline. Reconnect to refresh them.

  • Do I need an account?

    No. RateRadar has no accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising. It works the moment you open it.
